How the tiers are set: 27 seasons of NFL lines (1999 to today) show that a stats model's disagreements with the closing line win only about half the time, so LOCK, Strong and Lean come only from patterns that beat the line year after year: Unders in 15+ mph wind (LOCK), playoff underdogs the model also likes (Strong) and Unders in 10-14 mph wind (Lean). Model leans show where this season's numbers disagree with the market, and are unproven. Grades and projections use only this season's games. No pick is a sure thing.

Record by tier

Every pick the dashboard shows, from model leans up, is saved before kickoff and graded after the game at the number it was called at. Units assume a 1-unit bet at standard -110 prices, where 52.4% breaks even. CLV (closing line value) is how many points better our number was than the line at kickoff: a few weeks of wins can be luck, but beating the close week after week rarely is.

What's working

Every Tuesday the week's picks are graded and archived, then split by what the dashboard knew when it made them. Records under 30 picks are mostly luck, so they're grayed out.
